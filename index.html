A Better Question

For years, digital wellbeing apps have asked: "How addicted are you?" But research suggests this might not be the right question.

The finding: In 2020, we compared validated questionnaires to actual iPhone Screen Time data. "Addiction" scores showed weak correlation (r = .12). Simple frequency questions correlated well (r = .54). People weren't in denial - but the distress they felt wasn't the same as how much they used it.

So we're asking something different: Is technology helping you live well?

How the field has evolved
2012
Addiction scales adapted from gambling research
2020
Research questions what these scales actually measure
2023
New "flourishing" measures validated (Janicke-Bowles et al.)
2026
beó tests whether microhabits improve perceived control

What we're testing: Do simple reconnection habits - breathing, movement, nature, connection - help people feel that technology is serving them rather than controlling them? We measure this weekly, plus a validated scale at the start and end. We share what we find - including what doesn't work.

We use the latest validated measures (2023) because the field has evolved. We pre-register our hypotheses so you know we're not cherry-picking results. And we acknowledge our limitations: this is an exploratory study with self-selected participants.

Sources: Janicke-Bowles et al. (2023) | Hendry (2020, n=82) | Andrews et al. (2015) | Parry et al. (2021)

How the research works

What you'll do

Download the app. Answer 6 baseline questions (3 mins). Each day, you'll see a microhabit - breathing, movement, nature, connection. Once a week, we ask one question: how in control did you feel? After 4 weeks, we ask the baseline questions again. That's it. No lockouts, no guilt, no scary numbers.

What we measure

  • How in control you feel each week (0-10)
  • Which microhabits you try and return to
  • Whether active engagement (10+ microhabits) relates to feeling better
  • Your digital self-control at start and end (validated scale)

Who can participate

Anyone 18+ curious about their relationship with technology.

Timeline

Research launches February 2026. First findings published Easter 2026. Pre-registered study design - we share what works AND what doesn't. Our goal: help people feel in control of their relationship with technology.

Privacy & ethics

How your data works

Email signup → we notify you at launch → you download app → app generates random ID → research data uses only that ID. Your email is deleted from our signup list once you're in the study. Before deletion, you can request removal.

Data stays on your phone

We receive only: which microhabits you tried (anonymised), your weekly control rating (0-10), and aggregate usage patterns. Never linked to your identity.

You can withdraw anytime

Before anonymisation, email us to delete your data. After your email is deleted and only your random ID remains, we can't identify you to delete - that's the point. You control when to stop using the app.

We don't sell your data

We don't share with advertisers. Your data stays with beó. We use Google Analytics for website stats (anonymised, no personal data).

GDPR & global privacy

Lawful basis: legitimate interest (research). Privacy by default. We process minimal data and anonymise as early as possible.

Research ethics

Following British Psychological Society Code of Human Research Ethics. Voluntary participation. Informed consent.
