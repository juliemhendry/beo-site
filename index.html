AI Governance and Measurement

Oversight you can evidence, not just assert.

beò helps organisations show that their AI systems are overseen by humans in fact, not just on paper, with the measurement rigour of a research practice.

What we do

Three ways we work

Governance claims are measurement claims. We treat them that way: with instruments, evidence, and published methods.

Oversight assessment

We assess whether the people accountable for an AI system can actually detect when it is wrong, interpret what it is doing, and intervene in time, and we document it as evidence.

Cognitive-impact measurement

Longitudinal measurement of how AI adoption changes the way a workforce thinks, decides, and collaborates, using validated instruments rather than satisfaction surveys.

Governance-claim evaluation

Research-grade scrutiny of the claims made about an AI system’s safety, fairness, or human control: do the instruments behind them measure what they say they measure?

beòLAB · Research

We publish what we find, including the null results.

beòLAB is our research arm. Every study is pre-registered on the Open Science Framework before data collection, and the findings are published whether or not they confirm the hypothesis. The discipline is the point: an instrument that does not measure what it claims produces governance that does not govern.
