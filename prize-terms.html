Prize Draw Terms

Last updated: 5 February 2026

Summary: Complete our 4-week research study for entry into a prize draw. Three winners will receive Amazon vouchers (£150, £100, £50 or local currency equivalent). No purchase necessary. Open globally to participants aged 18+.

1. The Promoter

This prize draw is operated by beó LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, trading as beó.

Contact: lab@beo.llc

2. Eligibility

Who Can Enter

  • You must be 18 years or older at the time of entry
  • Open to participants worldwide, except where prohibited by local law
  • You must have access to an iOS device (iPhone or iPad) to use the app
  • Employees of beó LLC and their immediate families are not eligible

Excluded Jurisdictions

Due to local regulations, residents of the following locations may not be eligible:

  • Quebec, Canada (requires registration with Régie des alcools)
  • Any jurisdiction where such prize draws are prohibited

If you are unsure whether you are eligible, please contact us before participating.

3. How to Enter

No Purchase Necessary

Entry to this prize draw is free. No payment or purchase is required.

Entry Method

To enter the prize draw, you must:

  1. Download the beó app (free) from the Apple App Store
  2. Complete the initial Self-Control assessment (approximately 2 minutes)
  3. Provide consent to participate in the research study
  4. Use the app over the 4-week study period
  5. Complete at least 3 of the 4 weekly check-ins
  6. Complete the final Self-Control assessment at study end

One entry per person. Multiple entries will be disqualified.

Study Period

  • Start date: 10 February 2026
  • End date: 10 March 2026
  • Draw date: 17 March 2026

4. The Prizes

Prize Value (GBP) Quantity
First Prize £150 Amazon voucher 1
Second Prize £100 Amazon voucher 1
Third Prize £50 Amazon voucher 1

Total prize pool: £300

International Winners

Winners outside the UK will receive Amazon vouchers in their local currency at the equivalent value (based on exchange rates at the time of the draw), or an alternative voucher of equivalent value if Amazon is not available in their region.

Prize Conditions

  • Prizes are non-transferable and non-exchangeable
  • No cash alternative is available
  • Vouchers will be sent electronically to the email address provided during registration

5. Winner Selection

Draw Process

Winners will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries using a computerised random number generator. The draw will take place within 7 days of the study end date.

Odds of Winning

The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. With an expected 200 completers, odds would be approximately:

  • First prize: 1 in 200 (0.5%)
  • Any prize: 3 in 200 (1.5%)

Notification

Winners will be notified by email within 14 days of the draw. If a winner does not respond within 14 days of notification, the prize may be forfeited and an alternative winner selected.
