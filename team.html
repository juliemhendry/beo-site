Julie Hendry

CTO

MSc Psychology (Distinction, 2020), BSc Computer Science, Fellow British Computer Society, Fellow Royal Society of Arts.

Julie identified the perception gap problem through academic research: in her 2020 MSc study, self-reported smartphone addiction measures failed to correlate with actual iPhone usage data, even though social media use measures did. This finding challenged existing addiction-focused approaches and revealed a fundamental issue: users' beliefs about their behaviour don't match reality.

With 20+ years building enterprise technology, she now applies research rigour to digital wellbeing. beó is the natural evolution of this work: building tools to test whether closing the perception gap, understanding design patterns, and choosing evidence-based interventions can help users reclaim real life.
